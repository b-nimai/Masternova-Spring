⚡Course Upload Tips

  • Set a price, or make the course free: either way, confirm it.
  • Describe the course in at least 50 characters: it shows on the course page.
  • The curriculum step is where you create and organise sections and lectures.
  • Add at least one section, and at least one lecture in every section.
  • Mark at least one lecture as a free preview, so visitors can try the course.
  • Upload a video for every video lecture; it must finish processing before you submit.
  • Every change saves by itself. Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z undo and redo curriculum edits.
